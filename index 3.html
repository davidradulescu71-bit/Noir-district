The idea behind the label

Quiet design.
Clear identity.

NOIR DISTRICT is a streetwear concept for people who like versatile essentials and graphic pieces that feel personal. The first collection keeps the palette simple and focuses on fit, comfort and original artwork.

Launch content kit

5 short-form video ideas

01 — “Fit check: one tee, 3 looks”

Hook: “One oversized tee. Three different moods.” Show three quick outfit combinations, with on-screen text for each look.

Caption: “One essential, endless combinations. Which fit is yours: 1, 2 or 3?”

02 — “From sketch to streetwear”

Hook: “What if a blank tee became the whole fit?” Show a timelapse of your own design, then a mockup and styled outfit.

Caption: “Concept 002 is taking shape. Original art, everyday uniform.”

03 — “Black vs stone”

Hook: “Pick your side.” Cut between black and stone tees with matching pants and shoes.

Caption: “Black or stone? Comment your pick. #StreetwearStyle #FitCheck”

04 — “Details matter”

Hook: “The little details change the whole fit.” Close-ups of print placement, fabric texture and silhouette. Use actual product samples before making quality claims.

Caption: “Simple base. Intentional details. NOIR DISTRICT.”

05 — “Build the first drop with us”

Hook: “Help choose our first colourway.” Show two original design options and invite viewers to vote.

Caption: “You choose the direction of Drop 001. A or B?”
